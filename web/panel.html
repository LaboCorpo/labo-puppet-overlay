    Labo test

    Experiments to make streaming easier. Each one is off until you turn it on; tell the crew what works.

    📈 Hype detector

    When your chat suddenly speeds up, a timestamp (and a clip) is made on its own: you will find the moment in Clips.

    🎁 Channel points → BabaDeck

    A viewer redeems a reward: the BabaDeck button you choose is pressed (scene, sound, message…). Affiliate or Partner.

    🙏 Thank the raids

    A message in your chat when someone raids you. {name}, {login} and {viewers} are replaced.

    💧 Break reminder

    While you are live, a reminder to drink and stretch.

    🩺 Stream health alerts

    With OBS connected (BabaDeck tab): a notification when frames drop or the network chokes.

    ⏳ “Starting soon” countdown

    A Browser Source for your waiting screen. Change the minutes and the text in the link.

    Chat bot

    It answers in your Twitch chat with your own account (log in with Twitch in HUD Twitch → Stream widgets), while LaboHub is open.

    🤖 Your commands

    In the answer: {name} the viewer, {target} the word after the command, {uptime}, {game}, {title}, {random:a|b|c} one at random.

    ⏰ Timers

    A message every N minutes while you are live, only if the chat moved in between.

    🧰 Ready-made commands

    👋 Welcome newcomers

    A message for someone who writes in your chat for the very first time (Twitch tells us).

    🔊 Sound commands

    Viewers type !name to play a sound on stream (where your alert sounds play). !sons lists them.

    Viewer games

    Games your viewers play from the chat. Each one has its own Browser Source for OBS (“Preview” shows it with fake data).

    🎉 Giveaway

    📊 Chat poll

    Viewers type the number of their answer (or !vote 2).

    🔢 Counters

    Deaths, wins, fails… !death shows it, !death+ / !death- changes it (moderators by default). Also from the BabaDeck.

    🎮 Viewer queue

    For “play with viewers”: they join with a command, you call the next one.

    🌧 Emote rain

    The Twitch emotes sent in your chat fall and bounce on your stream.

    🔥 Hype meter

    A bar that fills up when your chat speeds up (same measure as the hype detector in Experiments).

    💜 End screen

    “Thanks for watching” with today's numbers (messages, chatters, followers, subs, bits, raids).

    Streamer tools

    ✏ Title & category

    Change them without opening Twitch, and keep presets for your usual streams (also on BabaDeck buttons).

    📺 Ad warning

    Before an automatic mid-roll: a chat message, a notification and a countdown on stream. Snooze pushes it back 5 minutes.

    📣 Discord: “I'm live”

    A message in your Discord server when your live starts. In Discord: channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New webhook → Copy URL.

    📈 Stream summary

    Counted while LaboHub is open; a notification sums it up when your live ends.

    Discord bot
    
      
    Friends create their puppet in Discord, without installing anything: they type /puppet create, choose with menus and see the result live. Only one person runs the bot (the “host”): their LaboHub must be open.
    
      
    
        
        
    Status—
    
        
        
    
        
    
      
    
      
    Share the puppets with the crew
    
      
    
        
        
        
        
    
        
    Without a GitHub token, puppets made with the bot are saved on the host only (and each friend can still send their /puppet code). With it, they are added to the crew's shared file, which every LaboHub reads every 2 minutes. Tokens are stored encrypted on this PC.
    
      
    
      
    Set up the bot (once, by the host)
        
      
          
    1. On discord.com/developers/applications, open the LaboHub application (the one in Discord setup), then Bot → Reset Token, copy it and paste it above.
      2. 
          
    2. Still in Bot, no special intent is needed (leave the “Privileged Gateway Intents” off).
      3. 
          
    3. Click ➕ Invite the bot to a server and choose the Labo's server.
      4. 
          
    4. Tick Run the bot on this PC. In Discord, type /puppet create.
      5.

    Welcome to LaboHub
    
      
    Everyone in your Discord voice channel appears on your screen as a paper puppet that talks with them. Three quick steps.
    
      
    1. Connect Discord
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    2. Your avatar
    
      
    
      
    
      
    Send your code (or .puppet file) to whoever manages the crew's avatar file: your puppet then shows up on everyone's screen.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    3. Shortcuts
    
      
    
      
    Closing this window keeps the app running in the taskbar and the system tray. Right-click the tray icon to quit.
